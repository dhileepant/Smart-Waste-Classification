🔬 Computer Vision Pipeline Specs

1. Region-of-Interest (ROI) Targeting

The OpenCV engine continuously isolates a central region from the input video frame to eliminate background clutter and isolate the target item.

2. OpenCV Preprocessing & Normalization

Frames are converted from BGR to RGB color space, bilaterally filtered for noise suppression, resized to 224×224, and normalized to float32 range [0, 1].

3. High-Speed Asynchronous Inference

Forward passes execute through the CNN model in under 15ms, maintaining consistent 30 FPS video streaming throughput.

4. Color-Coded HUD Annotations

Live overlays render dynamic bounding boxes: Emerald Green for recyclables and Bright Red for hazardous waste with real-time confidence scores.
