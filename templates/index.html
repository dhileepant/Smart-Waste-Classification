Waste Category Reference Guide

♻️

Recyclable Waste

Designated Blue / Green Bins

Plastics, cardboard, clean paper, metal cans, glass bottles, and beverage containers suitable for industrial remanufacturing.

💡 Best Practice: Empty all liquids and flatten containers to optimize recycling collection efficiency.
⚠️

Hazardous / Non-Recyclable

Designated Red / E-Waste Centers

Lithium batteries, electronic circuits (e-waste), chemical solvents, pesticides, mercury bulbs, and bio-hazardous materials.

🚫 Caution: Never discard into standard trash. Heavy metals and electrolytes contaminate soil and water tables.
